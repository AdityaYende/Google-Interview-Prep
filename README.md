# Google Interview Preparation

## 1. Company Selected
Google

## 2. Hiring Process
The standard software engineering hiring process at Google typically follows these stages:
*   **Resume Screening:** A recruiter evaluates your background, skills, and alignment with the role. 
*   **Online Assessment (OA):** A timed coding challenge featuring algorithmic problems.
*   **Technical Phone Screen:** A 45-60 minute video call where you solve a medium-to-hard coding problem in a shared Google Doc, requiring you to explain your time and space complexity out loud.
*   **Onsite / Virtual Loop:** Usually four to five back-to-back interviews covering extensive coding, system design, and a behavioral round focused on "Googleyness" (culture fit, teamwork, and handling ambiguity).
*   **Hiring Committee & Team Matching:** A committee reviews your interview feedback, followed by interviews with engineering managers to find a specific team fit.

## 4. Interview Preparation Tips
*   **Master the Environment:** Google often uses plain Google Docs or basic text editors for interviews. Practice writing your Java code without relying on syntax highlighting or autocomplete.
*   **Prioritize Core DSA:** Focus heavily on graphs (BFS/DFS), trees, and dynamic programming, as these are the heart of Google's technical rounds.
*   **Communicate Clearly:** Always explain your thought process out loud before and during coding.
*   **Prepare for Behavioral:** Use the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method to structure your answers for the Googleyness and teamwork rounds.

## 5. Any Other Useful Information
*   **Mock Interviews:** Regularly schedule mock interviews to simulate the pressure of back-to-back onsite rounds.
*   **Optimize Working Solutions:** Google interviewers favor complex, multi-part problems where you are asked to optimize a working solution rather than just solving it once.
